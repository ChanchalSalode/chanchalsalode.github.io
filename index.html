Research Interests

Research Projects

Intermodal Hub Location Problem, Sponsored by CRIS, Ministry of Railways, Government of India

March 2025 to March 2026
Centre for Railway Information Systems (CRIS), India

  • Built large-scale MILP models and solution methods for intermodal hub location and freight routing across the Indian Railways network, under capacity, budget and service-level constraints.
  • Applied MiniBatch K-Means clustering to aggregate origin-destination demand data at a scale of millions of points, producing representative clusters that cut memory use and solve time while preserving the structure of the demand.
  • Developed Benders decomposition and acceleration strategies that brought country-scale instances inside practical time limits.
  • The project aims to enhance efficiency, capacity utilization, and multimodal integration within the Indian Railways network through intelligent infrastructure planning.
