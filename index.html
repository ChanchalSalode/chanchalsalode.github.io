I build large-scale optimization models for the design and operation of freight and transportation networks. My doctoral work, advised by Prasanna Ramamoorthy at IIT Delhi, covers hub location, intermodal terminal siting, protection of networks against disruption, and scheduling, solved with exact decomposition methods on national-scale instances. Research

My work sits in large-scale combinatorial optimization, with network design as the recurring problem class. I develop exact algorithms, mainly Benders decomposition and branch-and-cut strengthened with valid inequalities, for hub location, intermodal terminal siting, critical infrastructure protection, and multilevel problems where an adversary or a disruption acts on the network after it is built.

The practical constraint in all of this is scale. Realistic instances carry demand data at a scale of millions of origin-destination pairs, which no exact method handles directly. I use machine learning to reduce that burden without discarding the structure that matters: MiniBatch K-Means to aggregate demand into representative clusters, and nearest-neighbour instance reduction to prune candidate routes ahead of the solver. Applications so far have been in rail and multimodal freight, and the same machinery carries over to supply chain and energy network planning.

Projects

Intermodal hub location and network planning for Indian Railways

Sponsored by CRIS, Ministry of Railways, Government of India March 2025 to March 2026

  • Built large-scale MILP models for siting intermodal hubs and routing freight flows over a national infrastructure network, under capacity, budget and service-level constraints.
  • Applied MiniBatch K-Means clustering to aggregate origin-destination demand data at a scale of millions of points, producing representative clusters that cut memory use and solve time while preserving the structure of the demand.
  • Developed Benders decomposition and acceleration strategies that brought country-scale instances inside practical time limits.
  • Worked with the operator's planning and data teams to reconcile model assumptions with field data and operating rules, and to present results in a form usable for investment decisions.
