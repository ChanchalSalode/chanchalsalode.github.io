Chanchal Kumar Salode

I’m a final-year PhD student at Indian Institute of Technology Delhi (IIT Delhi, India), advised by Prof. Prasanna Ramamoorthy. My research lies at the intersection of operations research, optimization, and artificial intelligence. I specialize in developing large-scale mathematical models (Mixed-Integer Linear/Nonlinear Programming, MILP/MINLP) and AI-driven optimization algorithms for complex infrastructure and logistics systems. My current focus is on scalable decision-intelligence frameworks that integrate machine learning with optimization to support sustainable planning in transportation, logistics, and infrastructure networks.

Before starting my PhD, I worked as a Research and Academic Associate at Indian Institute of Management Indore (IIM Indore, India), under the supervision of Prof. Saurabh Chandra.

My long-term research vision is to build AI-driven decision-optimization systems that enable sustainable and intelligent infrastructure planning at a global scale.

Research Interests

I am interested in combinatorial optimization, large-scale optimization, and energy systems. My current research focuses on developing exact algorithms and scalable optimization methods for network design, critical infrastructure planning, and multilevel optimization, with an emphasis on decomposition techniques and large-scale mixed-integer optimization.

Research Projects

Intermodal Hub Location Problem, Sponsored by CRIS, Ministry of Railways, Government of India

March 2025 to March 2026
Centre for Railway Information Systems (CRIS), India

  • Built large-scale MILP models and solution methods for intermodal hub location and freight routing across the Indian Railways network, under capacity, budget and service-level constraints.
  • Applied MiniBatch K-Means clustering to aggregate origin-destination demand data at a scale of millions of points, producing representative clusters that cut memory use and solve time while preserving the structure of the demand.
  • Developed Benders decomposition and acceleration strategies that brought country-scale instances inside practical time limits.
  • The project aims to enhance efficiency, capacity utilization, and multimodal integration within the Indian Railways network through intelligent infrastructure planning.
